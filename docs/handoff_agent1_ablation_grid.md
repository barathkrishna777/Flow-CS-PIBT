## What the Results Will Tell Us

The completed ablation grid forms a 3×2 table:

|                     | ORCA shield | CV-PIBT |
|---------------------|-------------|-------------|
| Straight-line vel   | (existing)  | (existing)  |
| ORCA reactive vel   | (existing)  | **new #1**  |
| Flow v4b (learned)  | **new #2**  | (existing)  |

- **Column comparison**: shows CV-PIBT improves any policy, not just our model
- **Row comparison**: shows learned velocity helps under both shields
- **Key hypothesis**: "Flow v4b + ORCA shield" will likely perform similarly to "Straight + ORCA" on random maps (ORCA deadlocks regardless of how smart the preferred velocity is), confirming CV-PIBT is the essential mechanism

For Sets B and C: if ORCA + CV-PIBT also only gets 25–30% on these maps, it's a domain gap problem, not a model-specific regression.
